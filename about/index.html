About Hamid

Technical support, systems administration, and hands-on project work.

I'm Hamid Samadi, a Technical Support Analyst based in Richmond Hill, Ontario. I have direct Tier 1 and Tier 2 experience and more than eight years in customer-facing roles.

I like the investigative side of support: reproducing the issue, checking logs and configuration, narrowing down the cause, and explaining the solution in terms the user can follow.

Outside of support work, I build labs and small tools to deepen my skills in Linux, networking, cloud, APIs, and automation. Hamid Systems is the name I use for this portfolio and those projects.

My approach

How I work through a problem.

Listen and reproduce

Start with what the user is seeing, gather the right details, and reproduce the issue when possible.

Work methodically

Check the likely causes one at a time, use logs and configuration as evidence, and avoid unnecessary changes.

Confirm and document

Verify the fix, explain what changed, and leave notes that make the next issue easier to handle.
